# Module 0 — The RAMPED Training Program

> A 12-week ramp: an intensive 4-week bootcamp, then 8 weeks of supervised selling with certifications.
> After that, veterans move into an ongoing weekly skill program.
> Structure is 🛠 RAMPED. It is built on these 📌 30MPC principles: 4-week calendar mixing industry,
> product and execution · ~6 weeks of heavy immersion · Marcus Chan's 12-week ramp · "win the first 30
> days" · training leads · a buddy who sat on the interview panel.

---

## Program at a glance

| Phase | Weeks | Goal | Gate to move on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bootcamp** | 1–4 | Learn the frameworks; prove them in role plays | Certifications C1–C3 |
| **Supervised selling** | 5–8 | Live prospecting and discovery with coaching | C4 + first meetings booked |
| **Full ownership** | 9–12 | Full book, full cycle | C5–C6 + ramp targets |
| **Continuous** | 13+ | Keep veterans sharp | Quarterly recertification |

Every new hire gets these before day 1:
- A **buddy**: a peer who sat on their interview panel.
- A **personal success blueprint**: their goals, income target, and what President's Club means to them.
- A **road-to-President's-Club** tracker.

## Weeks 1–4: Bootcamp

Daily shape:
- AM: learn
- Midday: drill
- PM: apply

Each Friday ends with a certification or showcase.

### Week 1: Foundations (industry, ICP, product, the opener)

| Day | AM: learn | Midday: drill | PM: apply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Welcome, success blueprint, tools setup | — | Shadow 2 calls with buddy |
| Tue | Industry and buyer: our ICP, personas, the problems we solve | Build the Messaging Matrix for persona 1 ([template](04-templates-and-scorecards.md#messaging-matrix)) | Listen to 5 recorded winning calls |
| Wed | Product: the 3 problems → 3 workflows | Build the Messaging Matrix for personas 2–3 | Shadow a demo |
| Thu | [Module 1 §1.2](01-prospecting.md#12-the-cold-call-opener--pitch--objections): openers and the problem proposition | 20 role-play openers with buddy | Write your pitch for each persona |
| Fri | **C1: Pitch certification**: deliver opener + pitch for 3 personas to manager | — | Week 1 retro |

### Week 2: Prospecting execution

| Day | AM: learn | Midday: drill | PM: apply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Mr. Miyagi objection handling | Objection gauntlet: 6 objections × 3 rounds | Research block: 40 accounts |
| Tue | Cold email ([§1.3](01-prospecting.md#13-cold-email)) | Write 4 emails for the sequence; peer review | Load first sequence (Tier 2 accounts) |
| Wed | Sequences, LinkedIn, video | Record 3 practice videos | **First live dial block** (40 dials, manager listening) |
| Thu | Territory plan ([§1.7](01-prospecting.md#17-territory-and-account-planning)) | Tier your book 1/2/3 | Dial block + call review |
| Fri | **C2: Cold call certification**: live role play with a surprise objection; a second attempt is allowed after feedback | — | Week 2 retro |

### Week 3: Discovery and the sales process

| Day | AM: learn | Midday: drill | PM: apply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 5-stage process and exit criteria ([§2.1](02-closing.md#21-the-5-stage-sales-process-buyer-agreements)) | Stage-quiz real deals from the CRM | Dial block |
| Tue | PPO agenda + Discovery Tree | Build a tree per persona | Shadow a top AE's discovery |

| Thu | Demo flow and reverse demo | Mock demo to buddy | Run first live discovery on a **training lead** (manager on the call) |
| Fri | **C3: Discovery certification** (rubric in [04](04-templates-and-scorecards.md#discovery-certification-rubric)) | — | Week 3 retro |

### Week 4: Late stage + showcase

| Day | AM: learn | Midday: drill | PM: apply |
|---|---|---|---|

| Thu | Forecasting and CRM hygiene | Lightning-review your own pipeline | Dial block |
| Fri | **Showcase week finale**: full-cycle mock deal (discovery → demo → negotiation) in front of the team | — | 30-day review with manager |

## Weeks 5–8: Supervised selling

- **Daily:** 2 dial blocks (power hours). Sequences run on the full Tier 2–3 book.
- **Weekly:**
  - Manager reviews 3 calls a week, chosen from the metrics.
  - The rep runs 2+ discoveries with the manager listening or reviewing the recording.
- **Training leads:** low-stakes inbound only. No strategic inbound until certification C4.
- **C4: Live call certification (end of week 6).** Two recorded live calls pass the rubric: one cold
  call and one discovery.
- **Targets:** first meetings booked by week 5; 4+ meetings/week (SDR) or first S2 opportunities (AE) by week 8.

## Weeks 9–12: Full ownership

- Full book, including Tier 1. Tier 1 accounts get "show me you know me" videos and multithreaded plans.
- AE: run full cycles. Close plans are reviewed live with the buyer.
- **C5: Deal review certification.** Present 2 live deals using the Deal Review template.
- **C6: Negotiation certification.** Live role play against the manager playing procurement.
- **Week 12:** 90-day review. Graduate to the full quota, or extend the ramp with a written plan.

## Week 13+: Continuous program (veterans)

Runs on the weekly rhythms in [Module 3 §3.6](03-leadership.md#36-operating-rhythms): skill lab, game
film, deal clinic, wins and losses.

🛠 Skill lab rotation (repeats every quarter):

| Week | Skill | Week | Skill |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Openers | 7 | Demo / reverse demo |
| 2 | Problem proposition | 8 | Multithreading / getting to power |
| 3 | Objections (dismissive) | 9 | Business case |
| 4 | Objections (situational / incumbent) | 10 | Close plans |
| 5 | Cold email teardown | 11 | Negotiation |
| 6 | Discovery tree + 5-Minute Drill | 12 | Territory plan for next quarter |

Quarterly **recertification**: every rep re-runs C2 and C3. Anyone scoring below 3 on any line gets a
2-week focus plan.

## Certification summary

| # | Certification | When | Pass bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Pitch (3 personas) | Week 1 | All rubric lines ≥ 3 |
| C2 | Cold call role play | Week 2 | ≥ 3 on every line; improvement on second attempt |
| C3 | Discovery role play | Week 3 | ≥ 3 on every line, including the 5-Minute Drill |
| — | Showcase (full cycle) | Week 4 | Team feedback; no gate |
| C4 | Live calls | Week 6 | 2 recorded live calls pass |
| C5 | Deal review | Week 10 | 2 deals pass the lightning review with ≤ 1 "no" |
| C6 | Negotiation | Week 11 | No concession without a "get"; price delivered cleanly |

Rubrics: [`04-templates-and-scorecards.md`](04-templates-and-scorecards.md).
