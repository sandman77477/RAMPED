# Module 3 — Leadership

> Goal: hire, ramp, and coach a team that hits President's Club every year.
> Tags: **📌 30MPC** = sourced doctrine · **🛠 RAMPED** = our build on top of it.

---

## 3.1 Team design

- 📌 Your first sales hires should be **builders** who create the process. Later hires **follow** that
  process (Ep. 283, "How to Build the Perfect Sales Team, Role by Role").
- 📌 Give each manager about **8 reps**. Stretch to 10–12 only if you have to.
- 📌 **Don't hire a director until you have at least 3 managers.**
- 📌 Look to retain and promote your own people before hiring from outside. A clear SDR → AE path
  keeps your best people.

## 3.2 Hiring

### Bar

- 📌 Hire only on an **enthusiastic yes**. Never settle for average ("8 Lessons That Separate Good
  Sales Leaders from Elite Ones").
- 📌 **Each interview stage should test a different selling skill**, and the person running that stage
  should be strong at that skill (Lead Playbook Part 2).

### The 4-step AE interview process

📌 The four steps, from "Sales Hiring Secrets No One Talks About":
1. Recruiter screen
2. Hiring manager interview
3. Interview loop: one-on-one interviews plus a culture panel
4. A **"talk them out of it"** interview

📌 These feed a scoring system built so that only strong candidates get through.

🛠 **What each stage tests and who runs it**

| Stage | Owner | Tests | Exercise |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Recruiter screen (30 min) | Recruiter | Motivation, track record, logistics | Walk me through your stack rank and quota attainment, year by year |
| 2. Hiring manager (45 min) | Hiring manager | Deal craft, self-awareness, coachability | Deal story: your biggest win and your worst loss, step by step |
| 3a. Mock cold call (20 min) | Top SDR/AE | Prospecting under pressure | Cold call a role-player → get feedback → **call again** |
| 3b. Mock discovery (30 min) | Senior AE | Discovery tree, 5-Minute Drill | Discovery with a role-played buyer → feedback → **run it again** |
| 3c. Culture panel (30 min) | 2–3 peers, including the **future onboarding buddy** | Team fit, how they work | "How You Work" questions |
| 4. "Talk them out of it" (30 min) | Leader (VP/CRO) | Conviction, real fit | Lay out the hardest truths about the role; check they still want it with eyes open |

- 📌 **Coachability test.** Double back on the mock: *"your most coachable reps will nail the 2nd try"*
  (Sean Gentry, #190). 30MPC also uses a mock cold-call filter to test coachability and how candidates
  perform under pressure.
- 📌 **Put the new hire's onboarding buddy on the interview panel** (John Sherer, #264).
- 🛠 Scoring: each stage is scored 1–4 (1 = strong no, 4 = strong yes). Any 1 or 2 means no hire. The
  scorecard is in [`04`](04-templates-and-scorecards.md#ae-interview-scorecard).

### Outbound recruiting top 1% reps

📌 From Ep. 251, "Outbound Recruiting to Find the Best Sales Talent" (Armand):
- **Sell before you screen.** Don't grill outbound candidates. Ask them to brag about what sets them
  apart, and judge quality from that.
- **Mini-pitch framework** for the role:
  1. Your personal story
  2. Your team's philosophy
  3. What impressed you about *them*
- **Backchannel** through mutual connections. Connect with everyone at your company on LinkedIn so
  you have the paths.

🛠 Recruiting message:
> "{First} — saw you {specific achievement: #1 at X / promoted twice in 18 months}. I'm {Me}, I run
> sales at RAMPED. I {one-line personal story}; we run the team on {philosophy, e.g. 'coaching every
> week, not every quarter'}. Not sure you're looking, but I'd love 20 minutes to hear what you're
> proudest of. Open to it?"

### For candidates (and for your SDRs who want to get promoted)

📌 Playbook 9, "How to Land a Killer Sales Job":
- Build a **target list of 25 companies** that meet your criteria.
- Write a **résumé with specific numbers**.
- Come into the interview with a **point of view**.
- Cold call the hiring manager, and run the process like a sales cycle.

📌 Run your promotion like a deal. Map the stakeholders and how the decision gets made, and raise the
objections before your manager does (Sarah Brazier, #60).

## 3.3 Onboarding

### Principles

- 📌 *"Win the first 30 days or lose the next 300"* (Jonah Mandel, #305).
- 📌 Spend the **first ~6 weeks on heavy immersion**, then move to learning on the job (Lead Playbook,
  "How to Train Your Team").
- 📌 Use **Mark's assessment matrix** to decide how much training time a skill gets:
  - How hard is it to learn (cognitive load)?
  - How proficient does the rep need to be?
- 📌 Give new reps low-stakes **"training leads"** before they handle high-value inbound.
- 📌 Give every new hire a **personal success blueprint** and a **"road to President's Club"** routine
  (Marcus Chan, #331).
- 📌 30MPC's 4-week program balances **industry, product, and sales execution** training. It uses
  simulated mock calls and **SMB discovery loops**.
- 📌 Marcus Chan's 12-week ramp:

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations and role plays |
| 2 | Live calls |
| 3 | First discoveries; shadow a top AE |
| 4 | "Showcase week" |
| 5–12 | Full immersion and certification |

🛠 The full RAMPED day-by-day plan is in [`00-program.md`](00-program.md).

### Mark's assessment matrix applied

🛠

| | **Low proficiency needed** | **High proficiency needed** |
|---|---|---|
| **Low cognitive load** | Self-serve: docs, Loom (CRM hygiene, tools) | Drill until it's automatic (cold call opener, PPO agenda, 5-Minute Drill) |
| **High cognitive load** | Awareness only (deep technical/product internals) | Most instructor time: role plays and certification (discovery tree, objection handling, negotiation) |

## 3.4 Coaching

### Call coaching

- 📌 **Prep 30 minutes before each 1:1** to decide what you'll coach (Sean Gentry, #190).
- 📌 **Start with the metrics.** Use them to decide which parts of which calls to listen to. That's how
  you get through 10 calls in a 30-minute session.
- 📌 Prep reps on two things for each call: **what they want out of it, and how they plan to get it**.
  Pay attention to the **first 7 "golden" minutes**, where discovery is won.
- 📌 **Role play every critical conversation**, even if it's minutes before the call (Mark Nietzel, #309).

🛠 **Diagnosing from metrics**

| Metric is low | Listen to | Likely fix |
|---|---|---|
| Connect → conversation | First 10 seconds | Opener, tone |
| Conversation → meeting | Pitch and objections | Problem proposition, Miyagi |
| Meeting held → S2 | Minutes 0–7 and the last 5 | PPO agenda, discovery depth, 5-Minute Drill |
| S3 → S4 | Demo recap, stakeholder map | Power and champion |
| Win rate / discount | Negotiation calls | Give-for-get, price delivery |

### The 1:1

📌 SDR 1:1 agenda (from a 30MPC Lead episode):
1. The rep's agenda first
2. Pipeline review of the accounts they plan to book
3. One good call and one bad call
4. Career goals

📌 Use the **"How You Work"** questions to understand the person behind the seller (Shay Keeler, #183).

🛠 RAMPED weekly 1:1 (30 min):

| Minutes | Topic |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Rep's topics |
| 5–12 | Numbers vs. plan (activity → meetings → pipeline → bookings) |
| 12–25 | Coaching on **one skill**, using 2–3 call clips the manager picked before the meeting, plus a 3-minute role play |
| 25–30 | One commitment for the week. Career goal check-in (monthly) |

### Deal reviews

- 📌 Review deals **as a team** to spot blind spots.
- 🛠 Use the Deal Review template in [`04`](04-templates-and-scorecards.md#deal-review). Ask the
  questions, don't tell. "Who's the economic buyer, and when did you last talk to them?" beats "you
  need to multithread."

## 3.5 Pipeline reviews and forecasting

- 📌 **Lightning pipeline review** (Mark Nietzel, #309): test each deal against key criteria with
  **binary yes/no answers**.
- 📌 Require **weekly pipeline trackers**, where *"reps forecast and explain exactly how they'll build
  and progress pipe."*
- 📌 **SDR pipeline-generation forecasting** (Maya Connet, #208):
  - Marketing, SDRs, and account directors each have a top-of-funnel quota, rolled up bottom-up.
  - Forecast early in the quarter = meetings booked over the last 2 quarters × conversion rate.
  - Get half of the quarter's meetings booked early.
  - Measure SDRs on **qualified opportunities, not meetings**.

🛠 **Lightning review questions.** Answer yes or no only. Any "no" becomes an action item.
1. Has the buyer stated the problem in their own words? (S1)
2. Is there a dated why-now? (S2)
3. Have we met the economic buyer? (S3)
4. Is there a champion who passes the 3-part test?
5. Is the close plan shared and dated through signature?
6. Is the next meeting on the calendar?
7. Do we know the paper process: security, legal, procurement?
8. Have we had live contact in the last 14 days?

## 3.6 Operating rhythms

- 📌 To get a team prospecting: *"set daily expectations, put them in calendar invites in an operating
  rhythm, then get wildly tactical on how to do it"* (Shay Keeler, #183).
- 📌 30MPC recommends 4 weekly team rhythms to keep veteran reps sharp. The episode doesn't name
  them, so the 4 below are ours.
- 📌 Build dashboards a manager can read at a glance, telling a story rather than a spreadsheet. Run an
  emotional pulse check about every 6 weeks (Jonah Mandel, #305).

🛠 **RAMPED operating cadence**

| Rhythm | When | Length | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power hour (team call blitz) | Daily | 60 min | Team dials together; shout-outs in Slack |
| Forecast and pipeline call | Monday | 45 min | Lightning review, commit/best case |
| **Skill lab** (weekly rhythm #1) | Tuesday | 45 min | One skill; live role plays in pairs |
| **Game film** (weekly rhythm #2) | Wednesday | 30 min | Team breaks down one recorded call |
| **Deal clinic** (weekly rhythm #3) | Thursday | 45 min | 2 deals reviewed by the team |
| **Wins and losses** (weekly rhythm #4) | Friday | 20 min | One win and one loss: what we learned |
| 1:1s | Weekly | 30 min | See above |
| Pulse check | Every 6 weeks | 15 min | Engagement and morale, one on one |
| QBR and territory plan | Quarterly | 2 hrs | Plan vs. actual; next quarter's territory plan |

## 3.7 Leader do's and don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Hire on an enthusiastic yes | Hire "good enough" to fill a seat |
| Test coachability with a second try | Judge only the first attempt |
| Sell before you screen in outbound recruiting | Grill passive candidates |
| Win the first 30 days | Hand a new rep high-value inbound on day 3 |
| Coach one skill at a time, chosen from the metrics | Listen to whole calls at random |
| Run binary pipeline reviews | Ask "how do you feel about this deal?" |
| Put rhythms on the calendar | Rely on reps to self-organize prospecting |
| Keep about 8 reps per manager | Stretch a manager past 10–12 reps |
