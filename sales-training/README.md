# RAMPED Sales Training Manual & Program

A complete prospecting → closing → leadership training system, built on the frameworks taught by
**30 Minutes to President's Club (30MPC)** — Armand Farrokh, Nick Cegelski, and their guests
(Mark Kosoglow, Jason Bay, Sam McKenna, Charles Muhlbauer, Todd Caponi, Marcus Chan and others).

## The one-page version

**Prospecting**
1. Research in blocks, dial in blocks — research first, then ~40 dials in 60 minutes.
2. Open with context: *"heard the name tossed around?"* or a permission opener that owns the cold call.
3. Pitch the **problem**, not the product. One-sentence differentiator. Ask an interest question.
4. Handle every objection with **Mr. Miyagi**: don't fight it → acknowledge → clarify → wax back to the pitch.
5. Sequences: start with email, ~10 touches over ~30 days, one pain per email, voicemails point to email.
6. Emails under 125 words, interest-based CTA, no calendar links in cold email, "show me you know me."

**Closing**
1. Stages are **buyer agreements**, not meetings: Problem → Priority/Solution → Power/Evaluation → Value/Commercial → Vendor approval.
2. Every call: PPO agenda (Purpose, Plan, Outcome) → discovery → **5-Minute Drill** (do you want to buy / when / how).
3. Discovery Tree: situation → operational problem → executive impact. Humbling disclaimers + "to what extent…".
4. Get to power before you negotiate. Build the business case *with* your champion.
5. Customer-facing close plan reviewed live on every call (power, security, legal gates).
6. Negotiate: explain how price works, give it, stop talking. Flinch & trade — never give without a get.

**Leadership**
1. Hire only on an *enthusiastic yes*; each interview stage tests one skill; re-run the mock to test coachability.
2. Onboard hard for the first 30 days ("win the first 30 days or lose the next 300").
3. Coach from metrics → pick the calls; prep 30 minutes before every 1:1.
4. Lightning pipeline reviews: binary yes/no criteria + weekly rep-written pipeline trackers.
5. Put prospecting into calendar-invite operating rhythms. Role-play every critical conversation.
